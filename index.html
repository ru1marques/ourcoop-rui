BUILT BY ARTISTS, FOR ARTISTS AND CULTURAL WORKERS

*** OUR*COOP *** is an international cooperative of artists, cultural workers and researchers, created to support new ways of producing, sharing and sustaining artistic practice across Europe *

*****     ************     ****     **
*** ***********Rather than functioning as a fixed institution, OUR COOP operates as a collaborative infrastructure ** a space where knowledge, resources and experiences circulate between people, disciplines and territories * It brings together practitioners from different cultural contexts to collectively address the social, economic and structural challenges faced by the contemporary arts ecosystem **

Emerging from the *Shared Visions project, the cooperative explores alternative models of collaboration, ** mutual support ** and cultural production, with a particular focus on strengthening connections between the Western Balkans and the rest of Europe * Through research * events * publications * and shared initiatives * OUR COOP seeks to build lasting relationships that extend beyond the duration of the project itself *

** Why a cooperative model?

A cooperative model reflects the way we believe artistic practices can grow: through collaboration, shared responsibility and collective support * Instead of focusing on individual ownership, OUR COOP creates a space where knowledge, resources and opportunities can circulate between members *

By working cooperatively, artists and cultural practitioners can build stronger networks, develop sustainable practices and collectively respond to the challenges of the contemporary cultural landscape * The cooperative is not only a structure — it is a way of working, based on mutual trust, participation and shared values *
