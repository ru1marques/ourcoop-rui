    Art Fund: 2,400 OUR of distributable yield · illustrative cooperative-wide amount

    Member deposits generate yield. That yield forms this cycle’s Art Fund. Your ballot allocates it across registered projects; it does not draw from Reserve, Education, Solidarity or Production.

    Up to 100 points per member, regardless of deposit size. Full budgets cap allocations; projects must reach their minimum viable budget. This preview saves your ballot only and does not calculate a collective allocation.
