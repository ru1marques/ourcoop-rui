Participation rules · Articles 27–29

Registered participation: at least one primary group and at most three groups, after at least three months of regular attendance. Permanent groups: at least 5 members and Assembly approval. Temporary groups: at least 3 members and Assembly approval. Emergency groups: at least 3 members, Management Board confirmation and a 3-month mandate. These are statutory areas, not a verified list of constituted groups.
